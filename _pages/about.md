I am a third-year Computer Engineering undergraduate at [Gachon University](https://www.gachon.ac.kr), expected to graduate in February 2028. My interests include **Affective Computing**, **Multimodal AI**, and **Human-Centered AI**. As an undergraduate researcher in the PRML Lab, I participate in paper seminars and Deep Learning study sessions.

I enjoy understanding how models work and explaining what I learn. In LG Aimers, I led a team and coordinated members' schedules and experiments. As Academic Team Lead of A.ing, I have worked on Transformer study materials and a Discord learning support bot. Reviewing experiments with teammates and preparing materials for club members have given me practice expressing my understanding through code and explanations.
